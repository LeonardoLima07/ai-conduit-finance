# Contuit: AI Finance Copilot

Create a complete SaaS web application called Contuit.

Contuit is an AI-powered financial operating system for businesses.

The platform combines:

accounting automation

financial management

invoicing

tax calculation

AI business advisor

The goal is to create a product similar to QuickBooks, but much smarter and simpler.

Contuit should act as an AI Copilot for entrepreneurs, helping them understand their finances and make better business decisions.

The first target market is Brazilian small businesses and MEI, but the architecture must allow global expansion.

PROJECT NAME
Contuit

TAGLINE
"The AI Copilot for Your Business"

CORE IDEA

Contuit is more than accounting software.

It is an AI assistant that understands a company and helps it grow.

The system automatically:

• connects bank accounts
• categorizes financial transactions using AI
• generates invoices
• calculates taxes
• creates financial reports
• predicts cash flow
• analyzes expenses
• suggests business improvements

The platform should feel like having a personal CFO powered by AI.

AI BUSINESS AGENT

Each company that joins Contuit creates a custom AI business agent.

During onboarding the user provides a business briefing including:

• industry
• number of employees
• monthly revenue
• products/services
• average price
• main costs
• growth goals

The AI agent uses this information to provide personalized insights.

Examples:

"Your business can safely hire another employee."

"You can increase profit by adjusting prices by 7%."

"Your company will run out of cash in 42 days if expenses remain the same."
